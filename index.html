Terms & Privacy

Private beta terms

TheBellyDanceDressingRoom is currently a private beta for trusted testers. Post only dresses you own or are authorized to sell, use actual photos, describe condition and measurements honestly, and do not copy, scrape, or redistribute listings.

Sign-in is handled by Google through Supabase. Listing details, seller display names, payment-contact fields, feedback, reports, and uploaded photos are stored in Supabase so this test marketplace can function. Do not place sensitive personal information in public listing details.

Payments are arranged directly between buyer and seller. The app does not process, hold, guarantee, refund, or mediate payments during this beta.

Safety

Abuse and spam plan

  • Google sign-in is required to post, report, or save seller profile details.
  • Listings can be reported for fake photos, spam, harassment, unsafe payment requests, or misleading condition details.
  • Problem listings can be deleted or hidden from Supabase while reports are reviewed.
  • Beta testers should share the link only with trusted dancers until moderation is stronger.
